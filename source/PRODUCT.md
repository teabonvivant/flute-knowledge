# 長笛知識館
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A public Hong Kong Traditional Chinese flute knowledge library. The user requests a more professional, trustworthy, artistic and literary experience, preserving the completed content while redesigning the frontend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the existing 100 journal articles, 100 people, 30 topic guides, research records, source links, diagrams, downloads, search, filters, pagination, mobile navigation and display preferences. Preserve the existing React/Vinext architecture and Sites project identity. Current delivery is a complete local preview; the user's earlier preview-first instruction remains active.

## Brand Commitments
The user rejected the silver-blue oversized sans-serif redesign. On 2026-09-22 they approved the proposed combination of Yakumo Saryo's quiet Japanese classical atmosphere and Natsuhasha's literary reading layout. Use gentle white, ink-coloured text, Chinese Ming/Song type and natural-light flute imagery. Existing editorial text may remain unchanged.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing content and assets are in content/, data/, public/images/, and public/downloads/. Do not invent institutional credentials or endorsements. A pre-redesign SHA-256 baseline is retained in ../redesign-content-baseline.json.
